About PriceWise

Our Mission

PriceWise is a web-based product price comparison platform designed to help consumers make informed purchasing decisions. We aggregate product information and pricing from multiple stores, allowing you to easily compare offers and find the best available prices.

What We Offer

  • Comprehensive Product Catalog: Browse a wide range of products across multiple categories
  • Smart Search: Find products quickly with our case-insensitive search functionality
  • Advanced Filtering: Filter products by category, price range, and store
  • Flexible Sorting: Sort products by price or name in ascending or descending order
  • Detailed Comparisons: View multiple store offers side-by-side with best price highlighting
  • Responsive Design: Access PriceWise on any device - desktop, tablet, or mobile

How It Works

  1. Browse our product catalog or search for specific items
  2. Use filters to narrow down products by category, price, or store
  3. Sort results to find the most relevant options
  4. View detailed product information including descriptions and ratings
  5. Compare prices across multiple stores to find the best deal
  6. Click through to the store to complete your purchase

Scope and Limitations

Current Scope

PriceWise currently focuses on providing product price comparison for consumer goods across popular retail categories. Our platform includes:

  • Product browsing and search functionality
  • Category and price-based filtering
  • Store-specific filtering
  • Multiple sorting options
  • Detailed product information display
  • Multi-store price comparison
  • Best price identification and highlighting

Limitations

  • Sample Data: The product and pricing data displayed is for demonstration purposes only and may not reflect current market prices or real-time inventory.
  • No Checkout: PriceWise does not handle payments, checkout processes, or order fulfillment. All purchases are completed directly through the respective stores.
  • No Shipping Management: Shipping information and costs are managed by individual stores, not by PriceWise.
  • No User Accounts: Currently, PriceWise does not offer user accounts, wishlists, or price alerts.
  • Currency Limitations: Price comparison is currently limited to products with comparable currencies. Currency conversion is not supported.

Technology Stack

PriceWise is built using modern web technologies to ensure a fast, responsive, and accessible user experience:

  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript
  • Backend: Node.js with Express.js
  • Database: SQLite (development), PostgreSQL (production)
  • Architecture: RESTful API with three-tier separation

Accessibility Commitment

PriceWise is committed to providing an accessible experience for all users. Our platform includes:

  • Semantic HTML5 markup
  • Proper heading hierarchy
  • Accessible form controls with labels
  • Keyboard navigation support
  • Visible focus states
  • Meaningful alt text for images
  • Color contrast compliance with WCAG AA standards
